# Custom livery sheets

Drop a 1024×1024 PNG here named after the team id (`rossa.png`, `argent.png`, `taurus.png`,
`papaya.png`, `verdant.png`, `alpenne.png`, `wilhelm.png`, `hayes.png`, `kinetik.png`, `vector.png`).
It is drawn over the procedurally painted sheet when the car is built.

Layout of the sheet:

- rows 0–512: top view of the car, nose on the left, left-hand side of the car at the top
- rows 512–768: left side of the car, nose on the left, top of the car at the top of the band
- rows 768–1024: right side of the car, same orientation

Transparent areas keep the painted livery underneath.
