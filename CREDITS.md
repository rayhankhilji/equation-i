## Car model

The in-game car (`assets/models/ei-car-hi.glb`, `ei-car-lo.glb`, `ei-car-id.png`, `ei-car-ao.png`) is a
decimated, re-textured derivative of the **"F1 2022 Monopost"** 3D model created and owned by
**Peter Kiss** ([@peterkissdesign](https://instagram.com/peterkissdesign)), distributed as a freebie by
[The Pixel Lab](https://www.thepixellab.net/free-3d-model-f1-2022-monopost-car).

The original is licensed **CC BY-NC-SA** (Attribution, Non-Commercial, Share-Alike) and its readme asks that
the freebie not be shared or sold. This project is a private, non-commercial game for personal play. If you
host or share this repository, keep this attribution, keep the use non-commercial, and consider asking the
author for permission at the link above. The derivative files here are provided under the same CC BY-NC-SA
terms.

## Circuit data

The Silverstone, Monaco and Yas Marina centrelines, the Monaco and Yas Marina pit lanes, building footprints and heights,
the harbour, the marina and the Yas Island coastline, parks, lawns, roads and street trees are derived from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) data, © OpenStreetMap contributors,
ODbL 1.0. The Monaco elevation profile is hand-built from published course data.
