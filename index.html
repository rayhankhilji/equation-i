How to race
Accelerate, brake before the corner, turn in smoothly and stay inside the white lines. DRS opens in the two DRS zones when you are within 1 s of the car ahead at the detection line (from lap 2). ERS gives a short power boost and recharges under braking. Hold until the five red lights go out — a jump start costs 5 seconds.
